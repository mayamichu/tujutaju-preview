What connects all of these

One model.
Different depth.

I work with Process Communication Model® (PCM), a practical framework for understanding differences in perception, motivation, communication and stress.

You do not need to learn the whole model to benefit from it. Sometimes one conversation is enough to see a situation differently. Sometimes a team needs a shared language and more practice.

The format changes. The goal stays the same: more clarity, less unnecessary tension and more ways to reach different people.

Why PCM

A practical way
to understand
what sits behind
communication

PCM stands for Process Communication Model®.
It is a research-based model for understanding personality structure, communication, psychological needs and predictable stress patterns.

01

How people perceive the world

Some people naturally focus on facts and structure, others on people and feelings, values, reactions, action or imagination. The same message can therefore land very differently.

02

What keeps people engaged

Different people draw energy from different psychological needs. What helps one person feel motivated and involved may do very little for someone else — or even create friction.

03

What happens under stress

Under stress, communication patterns shift. People may become more controlling, critical, withdrawn, reactive or impulsive. PCM helps you recognise these changes earlier and respond more deliberately.

The goal is to have more than one way to reach them.

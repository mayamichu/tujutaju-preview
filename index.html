Why PCM

A practical way
to understand
what sits behind
communication

PCM stands for Process Communication Model®.
It is a research-based communication model rooted in psychological principles, with a methodology refined and validated through decades of research.

01

How people perceive the world

Some people naturally focus on facts and structure, others on people and feelings, values, reactions, action or imagination. The same message can therefore land very differently.

02

What keeps people engaged

Different people draw energy from different psychological needs. What helps one person feel motivated and involved may do very little for someone else — or even create friction.

03

What happens under stress

Under stress, communication patterns shift. People may become more controlling, critical, withdrawn, reactive or impulsive. PCM helps you recognise these changes earlier and respond more deliberately.

The goal is to have more than one way to reach them.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to know anything about PCM before we start?

No. You do not need any previous knowledge of PCM. We can start with a situation you are facing, with understanding your team better, or with a communication challenge you would like to solve.

Is PCM a personality test?

PCM uses a professional personality profile, but the value is not in assigning a label. The profile helps you understand how you perceive the world, what motivates you, how you communicate and what tends to happen under stress.

Is PCM only for workplace communication?

No. The same patterns appear at work, in families, relationships and everyday communication. The context may change, but the way people perceive, react and connect often follows the same underlying patterns.

Can I work with you individually?

Yes. Most of my training work is with leaders and teams, but I also work individually with people who want to understand a specific communication situation, a recurring pattern or themselves more deeply. From time to time, I also run open trainings that individuals can join independently of a company.

Do I need to start with a full training or personality profile?

No. We can start small. For a team, that may be a short practical workshop or introductory session. Individually, you can start with a consultation without profiling and go deeper if you find it useful.

What languages do you work in?

I work in Estonian, English and Russian.

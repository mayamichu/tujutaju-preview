PCM training gives teams and leaders a practical framework for understanding differences in communication, motivation and stress.

It helps people notice patterns earlier and choose a response that works better for the person in front of them.

Available in Estonian, English and Russian.

What the training is for

Less guessing.
More understanding.

People can hear the same words, need different things to stay engaged and react very differently under pressure.

PCM gives the team a shared way to read those differences. Instead of relying only on intuition, participants learn what to notice and what to do with that information.

The aim is practical: better conversations, fewer unnecessary misunderstandings and more flexibility when communication gets difficult.

What participants learn

Six perspectives.
More ways to connect.

Participants learn the core PCM logic and immediately apply it to real communication situations.

Recognise perception differences

Understand why people naturally focus on different aspects of the same situation and why the same message can land differently.

Adapt communication

Learn how to make a message easier for different people to hear without losing your own clarity or authenticity.

Understand motivation

See which psychological needs help different people stay engaged, resourceful and involved.

Spot stress earlier

Recognise predictable shifts in behaviour before tension escalates into recurring communication problems.

Respond more deliberately

Build more than one way to handle difficult conversations, feedback, pressure and disagreement.

Build a shared language

Give the team a practical vocabulary for discussing differences without turning them into personal judgements.

Individual profile

A personal map
inside a shared model.

PCM training can include the official PCM personality profile. The standard profile is 26 pages and gives each participant a personal view of their personality structure, communication preferences, psychological needs and stress patterns.

For leadership training, participants receive an extended leadership profile with additional material relevant to leading others.

The profile is not used to put people into boxes. It gives participants a more precise language for understanding their own patterns and recognising differences in others.

PCM profile and debrief

More than a result.
A practical personal map.

The PCM profile is not a test that puts you into one box. It shows the unique combination of six personality types present in you.

Together, we translate the profile into everyday language and connect it to situations you already recognise.

Clarity about your personality

Understand your personal structure and the strengths available to you.

Insight into communication

See how you naturally perceive, speak, listen and connect with others.

Motivation and stress patterns

Learn what helps you stay balanced and what changes when pressure builds.

Options for real situations

Apply the profile to conversations, decisions and relationships that matter to you.

How profiling works

Three steps,
with time to use
what you learn.

You receive a link to the official PCM questionnaire. Set aside approximately 45–60 quiet minutes to complete it online.

02

You receive your 26-page profile and we explore it together, online or in person in Tallinn, with practical links to your own experience.

03

A one-hour follow-up is included. We revisit what you have noticed in practice, answer new questions and deepen the practical use of PCM.

PCM Profile and debrief

Practical
Map
of yourself

Your profile doesn’t give you another universal list of things you should be doing. It helps you see what genuinely supports you — and give yourself permission to take your own needs seriously.

During the debrief, we connect the profile to your strengths, psychological needs, motivation and the way you respond under pressure. You leave with a personal framework you can return to in work, relationships and everyday life.

Use your strengths more consciously

See what comes naturally to you — and how to use more of your personality when a situation calls for something different.

Know what keeps you resourceful

Understand the psychological needs that support your motivation, balance and ability to be yourself.

Recognise stress earlier

Notice the first changes in your communication and behaviour before pressure pulls you into a familiar unhelpful pattern.

Have more choice in communication

Prepare for important conversations, adjust your approach and respond with more awareness instead of repeating the same reaction.

How profiling works

Three steps,
with time to use
what you learn.

01

Complete the questionnaire

You receive a link to the official PCM questionnaire. Set aside approximately 45–60 quiet minutes to complete it online.

02

Meet for the 2.5-hour debrief

You receive your 26-page profile and we explore it together, online or in person in Tallinn, with practical links to your own experience.

03

Return after one month

A one-hour follow-up is included. We revisit what you have noticed in practice, answer new questions and deepen the practical use of PCM.
